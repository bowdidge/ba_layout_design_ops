    • Schedule for 2027 PCR Layout Design and Operations meet

    Check closer to the event for the full schedule.

    Friday, February 5: Tour and Dinner

    1:00 pm - 5:00 pm: Rail-related tour in the East Bay.

    Location to be announced.

    6:00 pm - 8:00 pm: Group no-host dinner at nearby restaurant.

    Meet your fellow attendees for dinner at a restaurant in the Alameda area.

    Saturday, February 6: Clinics and Layout Tours

    8:45 am - 5:00 pm: Clinics at Mastick Senior Center, 1155 Santa Clara Ave., Alameda, 94501. (map)
    9:00 am - 2:00 pm: Layout Design and Operations consultations at the Mastick Senior Center.

    Sign up for a consultation session to get advice on your model railroad layout design or operations scheme from an experienced modeler. Learn more about signing up for a design consulation.

    Sunday, February 7: Operating Sessions

    9:00 am - 5:00 pm: Operating sessions at nearby layouts.

    Interested attendees will get an invite to an operating session at a local layout. This is a great chance for interested folks to learn about model railroad operations, and also give experienced operators a chance to visit new layouts. Operating sessions will be assigned for interested attendees a few weeks before the event - make sure to list your interest when you purchase a ticket to the meet.

    Learn more about participating in an operating session during the meet.
